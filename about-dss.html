What is DSS

DSS (Decision Support System) is the centralized hub for Billing Dispute guides, tools, and resources at FedEx Freight. It brings together every reference a BD analyst needs — billing dispute scenarios, pricing policies, surcharge references, account handling procedures, and PAUD queue routing — into a single, searchable workspace.

We serve 200+ FedEx Freight employees across multiple queues, from PAUD and HOT to CRAU and FPAY. Whether you're submitting an EBS response, generating FBC comments, or looking up a rare surcharge code, DSS is designed to get you the right answer faster and more consistently.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Journey

Q1 2024

The problem identified

Billing dispute guides were scattered across email, Teams channels, and shared drives. New analysts spent weeks building their own reference library.

Q2 2024

First version shipped

An MVP with the core billing dispute guides launched to a small pilot group. Weekly feedback shaped what came next — the version you see today owes a lot to it.

Q3 2024

Tools added

The EBS Response Builder and FBC Comment Generator joined the suite — turning DSS from a reference library into an actual workflow tool.

Q4 2024

Directories & the Employee Directory

Legacy and Shine Case Directories went live, followed by the Employee Directory — so the team could find each other without leaving the tool.

2025 →

DSS v2

Redesigned end-to-end: 75+ guides, 200+ active users, unified search, profile personalization, and a much cleaner, faster experience.
